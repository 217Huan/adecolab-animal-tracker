# Listening sources

Supplementary Irodori listening worksheet, free to use on AdecoLab. Not GENKI recordings.

- Copyright: The Japan Foundation Japanese-Language Institute, Urawa.
- Official source: https://www.irodori.jpf.go.jp/starter/audio/lesson03.html (also lessons 04 and 09)
- Lesson transcript: https://www.irodori.jpf.go.jp/assets/data/starter/pdf/X_L03.pdf (also X_L04.pdf and X_L09.pdf)
- Usage guidelines checked 2026-11-18: https://www.irodori.jpf.go.jp/en/privacy.html
- These exercises accompany and link to Irodori, with attribution and free access. Audio is streamed unchanged from the official host; no copied audio files are distributed in this repository. Availability depends on the source host.
- Replaced elementary greetings with full conversations: 03-07 (names/countries, L3-6, 59.996s), 04-08 (family/age/home, L4-6, 58.615s), 09-03 (daily routine, L9-12, 32.995s). Durations measured from downloaded MP3s with ffprobe. Irodori lesson numbering is independent of GENKI. Japanese scripts are converted to kana; Chinese/English translations are learning aids. Speaker labels and all dialogue turns are retained. Source links update with each track.
- Speed options: 0.5, 0.75, 1, 1.25, 1.5. No autoplay. Selecting a different track or leaving listening pauses the current audio. No third-party player scripts.

# Listening sources

Supplementary Irodori listening worksheet, free to use on AdecoLab. Not GENKI recordings.

- Copyright: The Japan Foundation Japanese-Language Institute, Urawa.
- Official source: https://www.irodori.jpf.go.jp/starter/audio/lesson01.html
- Lesson transcript: https://www.irodori.jpf.go.jp/assets/data/starter/pdf/X_L01.pdf
- Usage guidelines checked 2026-11-18: https://www.irodori.jpf.go.jp/en/privacy.html
- These exercises accompany and link to Irodori, with attribution and free access. Audio is streamed unchanged from the official host; no copied audio files are distributed in this repository. Availability depends on the source host.
- Tracks 01-08, 01-09: greetings, printed page L1-3. Tracks 01-14 and 01-17: goodbye and good night, printed page L1-5. Japanese text follows the short dialogs; translations are learning aids. Transcripts display the whole exchange, not time-aligned cues.
- Speed options: 0.5, 0.75, 1, 1.25, 1.5. No autoplay. Selecting a different track or leaving listening pauses the current audio. No third-party player scripts.
